## Worth deciding on

- [ ] **Santana Row Master Plan Update** (San José): about 1.27 million sq ft more office in four buildings of 139
  to 162 ft on the 42.5-acre Santana Row site; revised notice of preparation Feb 13, 2026 (SCH 2013122059).
  Recommendation: **include next** (meets the bar, in formal review). [candidates-south-bay-2026-10.md]
- [ ] **98 Franklin Street** (Hub, next to One Oak): 36 stories, about 365 ft, 345 homes over a high school; approved
  2020, a development agreement for 400 ft and 385 homes still under review; site permit filed, not issued.
  Recommendation: **hold**; add if you want every 350–400-ft tower at Market and Van Ness. [candidates-sf-van-ness-market.md]
- [ ] **Gateway Crossings** (Santa Clara): 1,565 homes and a 225-room hotel on 21.4 acres, approved 2019; it lies
  inside the mapped Santa Clara Station Area plan. Recommendation: **your call** whether to draw a project inside a
  mapped plan. [candidates-south-bay-2026-10.md]
- [ ] **598 Brannan Street** (Central SoMa Key Site 5): three office buildings and an affordable building, about
  994,000 sq ft, approved 2019. **725–765 Harrison Street** (Key Site 2): a 185-ft, 981,000 sq ft office building,
  approved 2019, extended to March 2028. Recommendation: **hold**, each just under 1 million sq ft and mid-rise.
  [candidates-sf-central-soma.md]
- [ ] **5M** (Fifth and Mission): 688 homes (302 built) and 676,000 sq ft, partly built. Recommendation: **hold** for a
  separate pass; its 2015 approvals weren't reachable. [candidates-sf-central-soma.md]
- [ ] **SFMTA Potrero Yard**: 513 affordable homes over a rebuilt bus yard, about 1.24 million sq ft in all;
  demolition and asbestos permits in Sept–Oct 2026. Excluded on Oct 17 as one building, mostly a bus garage.
  Recommendation: **your call**, now that site work is starting. [candidates-sf-other-sites.md]
- [ ] **San José State University Campus Master Plan**: about 3.7 million gsf by 2045 on two campuses, approved by
  the CSU Trustees May 21, 2025. Recommendation: **borderline, later**: a long-range campus plan with little to draw.
  [candidates-south-bay-2026-10.md]
- [ ] **Almaden Office** (San José, Almaden Blvd and Woz Way): two 16-story towers, about 2.05 million sq ft,
  approved 2021; status unknown. Recommendation: **borderline**; a single-block project like Oceanwide.
  [candidates-south-bay-2026-10.md]
