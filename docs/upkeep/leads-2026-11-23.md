## New project leads

- [ ] **Apple North San José campus** (San José, Orchard Parkway at Component Drive)
  - Size: about 4,151,350 sq ft of industrial, office and R&D space on 86.35 gross acres (four sites Apple
    assembled); no homes. About 12% of the allowed floor area is built.
  - Stage: entitled (2016 development agreement, 15-year term to March 2031). New activity: a first amendment
    to the agreement (DA26-001) removes a 9.7-acre parcel for PG&E and, with two street vacations, lets Apple
    build one campus. The Planning Commission recommended it 8-0-3 on Oct 28, 2026; it was item 10.2 on the
    City Council agenda of Nov 10, 2026. The Council's vote wasn't confirmed (the API shows no action history).
  - Official: City Council memorandum, file 26-1159,
    https://legistar.granicus.com/sanjose/attachments/33a546b0-cfda-4031-8f27-5211a4e444f3.pdf
    - "The conveyance will not diminish Apple's ability to develop approximately 4,151,350 square feet of
      industrial/office/research and development use on the remaining subject property"
    - "Only about 12% of the allowed building area under the Development Agreement has been built to date"
  - Opened: yes (memorandum with the attached Planning Commission staff report).
  - Recommendation: **include, once the Council vote is confirmed**. Well over 1M sq ft on a defined site, and the
    only lead that moved this month. The catch: there are no current building plans to draw (the memo says Apple
    "intends to seek an amendment to the Master" permit), so it would be a boundary only.

- [ ] **County Administration Center Redevelopment** (Santa Rosa; Sonoma County is the lead agency)
  - Size: up to 1,500 homes in buildings up to seven stories, an approximately 250,000 sq ft County office
    building, a 7,000 to 13,000 sq ft emergency coordination center and up to 20,000 sq ft of retail, on 14.6
    acres of the County campus.
  - Stage: in environmental review. Draft EIR received July 15, 2026; comments closed Aug 28, 2026; Board of
    Supervisors hearing Aug 18, 2026. The offices are studied at project level, the housing at programme level.
    SCH 2024121124.
  - Official: https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2024121124/4 ; notice of availability
    https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2024121124/4/Attachment/WUGexa
    - "The project would also potentially provide up to 1,500 residential units and up to 20,000 square feet of
      retail/commercial space."
    - "Construction could begin as early as 2027"
  - Opened: yes.
  - Recommendation: **include**. A public, multi-building redevelopment over the homes bar, beside a SMART
    line and with a draft EIR to draw from. The housing has no building layouts yet.

- [ ] **River Oaks Station transit-oriented development** (San José, North First Street at River Oaks Parkway;
  VTA)
  - Size: VTA's full build-out vision is 1,095 homes, 333,150 sq ft of office and 20,990 sq ft of commercial
    space on the roughly 18-acre VTA headquarters and park-and-ride site. Phase 1 (Eden Housing) is 328 homes.
  - Stage: proposed. VTA's board approved a partnership with Eden Housing for the first phase in October 2025;
    a community open house was held Jan 31, 2026; the access study is dated Mar 25, 2026. No City application
    found.
  - Official: https://www.vta.org/projects/river-oaks-station-transit-oriented-development ; access study
    https://www.vta.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/VTA-RiverOaks-Final-Report-3-25-26.pdf (p. 23)
    - "VTA envisions that the full build-out of the TOD site will include 1,095 residential units, 333,150
      square feet of office space, and 20,990 square feet of commercial space."
  - Opened: yes.
  - Recommendation: **borderline, leaning include**. Just over the homes bar and the same kind of agency-land
    project as the six BART station sites; the study's Figure 9 shows the developer's concept. Not new this
    month. The other VTA sites opened (Milpitas, 28th Street/Little Portugal, Downtown San José, Berryessa,
    Santa Clara) are 1.7 to 12 acres with no programme stated.

- [ ] **Harvest and Lombardo Ranch master plans** (Dixon, Solano County)
  - Size: Harvest, up to 6,300 homes with commercial and mixed-use space; Lombardo Ranch, up to approximately
    825 single-family homes (147 acres). The notice of completion gives 7,125 homes and 806,258 sq ft of
    commercial. The annexation is about 2,033 acres inside a 4,233-acre project area; build-out over 20 to 25
    years.
  - Stage: in environmental review. Revised NOP June 26, 2026 (comments to July 27, 2026; scoping July 13,
    2026). The first NOP, July 21, 2025, had up to 8,752 homes. SCH 2025070838.
  - Official: https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2025070838/3 ; NOP
    https://ceqanet.lci.ca.gov/2025070838/3/Attachment/sdMAss
    - "a phased, mixed-use community of up to 6,300 residential units"
    - "up to approximately 825 single-family residential dwelling units"
  - Opened: yes.
  - Recommendation: **borderline**. By far the largest new housing proposal found, but a greenfield annexation
    on the region's edge, mostly low-density. If `suisun-expansion` is the precedent, include; if the map is
    about urban sites, leave out.
