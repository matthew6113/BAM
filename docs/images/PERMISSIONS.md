# Image permissions: outreach list

Matthew's list for asking permission to show renderings, and photos where no usable open photo
exists or the only one is old. Built from the four research files in this folder
(`candidates-sf-east.md`, `candidates-sf-west-islands.md`, `candidates-peninsula-south-bay.md`,
`candidates-east-north-bay.md`) and `data/photos.json`. Nobody has been contacted yet. Prepared
2026-10-06.

## Why we need permission

The map's rule is that renderings appear only with the owner's permission or when Matthew made
them. Otherwise the panel links to the official page. Renderings belong to the developer or
architect, even when an agency has them in a staff report, plan or EIR, so finding one in an
agency document doesn't give us the right to show it. The same goes for any photo that isn't
under an open licence.

What to ask for, in writing:

- Permission to show the named images (list each one by title or page) on a free, non-commercial
  public portfolio map at https://matthew6113.github.io/BAM/, with credit.
- The exact credit line they want, for example "Rendering: Studio name for Developer name".
- Whether resizing and cropping to fit the panel is OK.
- Whether the permission ends at any point, and any other conditions, such as labelling the
  image "conceptual" or removing it if asked.

What to keep:

- Save the whole reply (PDF or the original email) somewhere permanent. Matthew decides where.
- Record the grant in `data/CREDITS.md` before anything is used: who granted it, the date, which
  images, the credit line, the conditions and any expiry.

## Contacts

One email can cover several projects, so this table is grouped by contact. Contacts are copied
from the research files exactly as they appear there. "Confirm first" means the research file
says the contact came from a search result, an old page or a page that couldn't be opened, so
check it before writing. Image locations are page URLs only. Never link or copy image files
directly.

Order: tier 1 covers the projects with no photo at all. Tier 2 covers projects whose only photo
is old or weak. Tier 3 covers the rest, which need renderings only.

I'll only use images you approve, I'll host them with the site rather than linking to your

if you could point me to them.

Thank you,

Matthew Huguet

## Before anything is used

- Never use an image until the written grant is saved and recorded in `data/CREDITS.md`. A phone
  call or a "sure, go ahead" without the specifics isn't enough; get it in writing.
- Never hotlink. Copy approved files into the build.
- Show the exact credit line the owner asked for, and follow any conditions (labels, crops,
  expiry).
- Don't show superseded designs as current, even with permission (for example the 2019 Related
  Santa Clara renderings or the 2015-era Parkmerced renderings).
- Update the Status column as you go, for example "[x] Asked 2026-10-12", then "Granted",
  "Declined" or "No reply".
