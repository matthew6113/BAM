# Image candidates: sf-west-islands group

Research only. Nothing was downloaded into the repo and no one was contacted. Checked 2026-10-06.

Projects: treasure-island, stonestown, balboa-reservoir, parkmerced, brisbane-baylands,
alameda-point, mare-island, oakland-coliseum.

How licenses were checked: each Wikimedia Commons file below was read on its own file page
(license template, author, date, source). Each Flickr photo was read on its Flickr page
(license link, date taken, owner). NC and ND licenses and "all rights reserved" images were left out.
Credit lines follow the TASL pattern (title, author, source, license). For CC BY and CC BY-SA, the
credit must name the author, link the license and say whether the image was changed. CC BY-SA also
requires that any edited version, such as a crop, be shared under the same license.

"Current" means the photo matches conditions on the ground today as far as we can tell. "Old" means
the site has changed since the photo was taken.
