# Image candidates: peninsula-south-bay group

Research only. Nothing was downloaded into the repo and no one was contacted. Checked 2026-10-06.

Projects: willow-village, parkline, related-santa-clara, downtown-west, north-bayshore,
moffett-park, the-rise, middlefield-park, tasman-east, berryessa-flea-market.

How I searched:
- I ran a geographic search on Wikimedia Commons for every geotagged file inside each project's
  traced boundary (`data/boundaries/<id>.geojson`). I checked each camera location against the
  polygon, so "on site" below means the camera was inside or within about 30 m of the boundary. I
  also ran keyword and category searches on Commons (for example "Vallco", "SRI International",
  "San Jose Flea Market", "Diridon", "Googleplex", "Levi's Stadium", "Lick Mill"), plus Openverse
  (license filter set to CC0, PDM, CC BY and CC BY-SA, which also covers Flickr).
- For every image listed, I read the license template, author, date and source on the file's own
  page (through the Commons API: wikitext and extmetadata). For the Flickr item, I read the Flickr
  photo page. I opened a small preview of each key image to confirm what it shows; where I couldn't,
  the entry says "not visually confirmed".
- I left out all NC, ND and "all rights reserved" images, plus photos where identifiable people
  are the subject (for example, Flickr `mliu92/35183662883`, "5827 Vallco", 2017: children inside
  the mall).

How credit lines work: they follow the TASL pattern (title, author, source, license). CC BY and
CC BY-SA need the author named, a link to the license, and a note if the image was changed. CC BY-SA
crops must stay CC BY-SA. CC0 and public-domain US government images need no credit, but giving one
is good practice. "Current" means the photo matches conditions on the ground today as far as I can
tell. "Old" means the site has changed since. Direct original URLs are for reference only. Don't
hotlink them: copy the file into the build if it's used.

Public-domain aerials for every project in this group. The USDA's 2022 NAIP orthophotos
(public-domain US government work, tagged `{{PD-USGov-NOAA}}` and `{{PD-USGov-USDA}}`) are on
Commons as quarter-quad GeoTIFFs of about 10,000 × 12,300 px. Each project's tile is listed below.
I confirmed the tile names on Commons, and the geosearch found the Moffett Park and Related/Tasman
tiles inside those bounding boxes. They are vertical map imagery rather than photos. They work for a
"site from above, 2022" inset (crop to the boundary), but the basemap probably already serves that
role. The data notice reads: "The USDA-FSA Aerial Photography Field office asks to be credited in
derived products". Suggested credit: "USDA-FSA Aerial Photography Field Office, NAIP 2022, via NOAA
Office for Coastal Management (public domain)". Category:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:2022_USDA_NAIP_4-Band_8_Bit_Imagery:_California

Some of the city pages below (menlopark.gov, sanjoseca.gov, santaclaraca.gov, mountainview.gov,
cupertino.gov, sunnyvale.ca.gov) blocked automated fetches (HTTP 403). I found their URLs by search
but couldn't open them. Check them in a browser before linking.
