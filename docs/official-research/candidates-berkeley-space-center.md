## The agreement in plain language

**What was signed, by whom, when.** The deal is a **lease of federal land**, not a sale and not a
university campus.
- **2019:** NASA invited the University of California to look at developing the site.
- **November 1, 2020:** the **UC Regents and NASA signed an Enhanced Use Lease** for about **36 acres** of
  the NASA Research Park at Ames. An enhanced use lease is a long-term lease of agency land to a private
  or non-federal party.
- **May 2021:** the Regents authorized UC Berkeley to form a joint venture with a private developer,
  **SKSP NRP, LLC**. The joint venture is **Moffett Partners, LLC**.
- **December 2022:** the lease was **assigned to an affiliate of Moffett Partners**.

Moffett Partners is the project proponent: it would build and run the campus. The EIR/EIS's preparers
list names **SKS Partners** as Moffett Partners' development manager. The EIR/EIS describes the lease's
date, its acreage and its assignment, and what it requires: housing priority for UC and federal people,
an environmental management plan and an EPA agreement. It does **not** give the lease's length or rent.
(FEIR p. 2-3 [PDF 205]; p. 2-34 [PDF 236]; p. 3.9-5 [PDF 632]; p. 8-5 [PDF 1420].)

**What it allows.** The land is in NASA's 2002 Ames Development Plan, which already provides for
research, education and housing in the NASA Research Park.

Moffett Partners has applied to NASA for a mixed academic and research campus of up to about **2.3
million sq ft**:
- labs, offices and academic space (2.0 million sq ft);
- a conference center;
- ground-floor shops, food and maker space;
- about **145 student and faculty housing units** and about **100 short-term lodging units**;
- at least **10 acres** of public open space around a Central Green facing Hangar One.

Buildings would be up to **80 ft** tall. The project would be built in four phases from **2027 to 2040**.

A smaller **1.4 million sq ft** version (the Reduced Density Alternative) was studied at the same level of
detail and could be approved instead. The Final EIR/EIS (p. 1-4 [PDF 186]) says:

> "The final decision as to whether to adopt the CEQA Proposed Project or the CEQA Reduced Density
> Alternative will be made after completion of the final joint Final EIR/EIS."

The University's response to comments says the project "would not be a satellite campus for UC Berkeley"
(p. 10-115 [PDF 1546]).

**Who decides.** There are two lead agencies:
- **UC (the Regents) is the CEQA lead agency.** It reviews the application and authorizes Moffett Partners to
  submit it to NASA.
- **NASA is the NEPA lead agency and the final decision-maker.** It owns the land and issues the building permits.

The EIR/EIS (p. 1-10 [PDF 192]) is explicit about this:

> "Certification of the joint Final EIR/EIS by the UC Regents as complete and adequate, in conformance with
> CEQA, does not grant any land use approvals or entitlements for the proposed project."
>
> "NASA is the final decision-making body regarding the project-specific discretionary development entitlements."

**Where it stands (Oct 19, 2026).**
- **Sept 16, 2026:** the University of California "authorized submittal of the above-described project to
  NASA". It adopted CEQA findings, a statement of overriding considerations and a mitigation monitoring
  program (NOD, filed Sept 17).
- **Next:** NASA's **Record of Decision**. The EIR/EIS puts it after the Regents' decision, the EPA filing
  of the Final EIS and a 30-day wait (Figure 1-1 [PDF 189]).
- **Then:** NASA design review and construction permits.

No NASA decision could be checked from this session.

Lead figures checked:
- "1.4M sq ft originally, ~2.3M now": both are official. The 2024 NOP already carried both as Build
  Alternatives 1 (2.3M) and 2 (1.4M), and the Final EIR/EIS keeps both. The 1.4M figure is the Reduced
  Density Alternative.
- "36 vs ~39 acres": 36 acres is the 2020 lease and the 2024 NOP's site. About 39 acres is the 2026
  EIR/EIS's Project Site, and 34.92 acres is the leased parcel boundary. The EIR/EIS doesn't explain the change; its
  Project Site line runs along the outer edge of the realigned Wescoat, Cody and Girard roads, outside the
  leased parcel line along Wescoat and Cody Roads.
- "Moffett Partners, authorized by the Regents in May 2021": confirmed (FEIR p. 2-3; NOP p. 2). SKS
  Partners is confirmed as development manager (FEIR p. 8-5).
- "Regents' September 2026 action": confirmed as an authorization to submit the project to NASA (Sept 16,
  2026), with findings, a statement of overriding considerations and a mitigation program. The NOD is
  signed by UC's Director of Physical and Environmental Planning. It does not say in so many words that the
  Regents certified the EIR, or at which meeting. The EIR/EIS anticipated that the Regents would "certify the
  joint Final EIR/EIS and then approve or deny the proposed project application" (p. 1-10).
- "NASA's NEPA decision": needed, and not yet shown. The Final EIS says "NASA’s decision will be published
  subsequently in a Record of Decision" (p. 4-1).

Stage: **`entitlement`**. The University's CEQA action is done (Sept 16, 2026). But the University's own
EIR says certification "does not grant any land use approvals or entitlements" and that NASA is "the final
decision-making body regarding the project-specific discretionary development entitlements". NASA's
approval and Record of Decision are still to come. The EPA covenant, which the lease requires, was in draft
in August 2026. If NASA's ROD has issued (check nasa.gov or the Federal Register), the stage becomes
`entitled`. Construction would then wait for NASA design review and permits.
