# Official-source findings: projects around the Caltrain railyard (Fourth and King)

Checked 2026-10-27. Matthew asked on 2026-10-27: "please locate all the projects happening around the cal
train railyard". The railyard is `sf-railyards` (Townsend, Fourth, King and Seventh streets; see
`candidates-4th-king-railyards.md`). Nothing here is on the map yet; this is a list to choose from.

How it was found: the Planning Department's Development Pipeline (DataSF, Q2 2026 snapshot published
Aug 28, 2026; CC0), every record within 1.2 km of the railyard's centre, then each larger one (100+ net
homes or 100,000+ sq ft) read in the Planning Department's project records (DataSF) for its current
status and description. Distances are from the pipeline's point for the project to the railyard's traced
edge, so they're approximate. Mapped projects were measured edge to edge from their traced boundaries.
`sfplanning.org`'s environmental review listing was searched for the Flower Mart and 88 Bluxome.
Unreachable from this session: `sfocii.org`, `sfport.com`, `caltrain.com`, `sfcta.org`, `tjpa.org`,
`citypln-m-extnl.sfgov.org` and `sf.gov`. CEQAnet was reachable, but its search ignored the queries.
Nothing from the press was read or recorded.

URL shorthand:
- `PIPE` = `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/6jgi-cpb4.json` (San Francisco Development Pipeline, "Snapshot
  of San Francisco's Development Pipeline as of the end of 2026 Q2"; query by `case_no` or `blklot`)
- `PRJ:<id>` = `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/qvu5-m3a2.json?record_id=<id>` (Planning Department
  Records – Projects)
- `ERD` = `https://sfplanning.org/environmental-review-documents?title=<words>`

## Left out, and why

- **395 Third Street** (400 ft, 522 homes): in the Q2 pipeline, but its record was "Closed - Withdrawn"
  on Sept 11, 2026 (`PRJ:2024-010155PRJ`).
- **598 Bryant Street** (25 stories, 353 homes, `2018-014043PRJ`, "Under Review" since 2019): not in the
  pipeline, and a 2024 re-filing (`2024-000212PRJ`, 380 homes, 31 stories) was cancelled.
- **The railyard itself** isn't in the Q2 pipeline snapshot (its application was opened in March 2026).
- **Not covered by the city's pipeline**: UCSF's Mission Bay campus (a state agency), the Port's projects
  other than Mission Rock, and infrastructure. The Pennsylvania Avenue Extension (SFCTA) and the I-280
  boulevard study are separate studies named in `candidates-4th-king-railyards.md`; their hosts were
  unreachable, so nothing about them was checked.

Matthew: "yes add all three". `655-4th-street`, `88-bluxome` and `flower-mart` are now mapped, all
entitled. Their boundaries are the key sites' lots from DataSF parcels
(`pipeline/bam_pipeline/sites/boundary_central_soma_key_sites.py`), checked against each record's site area.

  and the pipeline give 1,096 homes and the description 1,105.
- 88 Bluxome: the 2019 office project (`2015-012490`, Alexandria Real Estate): Motion 20493, office towers of
  225 and 185 ft with 839,860 gsf of offices, environmental review closed Sept 18, 2019, modified in 2023 to

  environmental record, and a transportation plan still under review. The pipeline marks it "PL Approved"
  but not as holding an approved entitlement.
- Flower Mart: owner KR Flower Mart, LLC (`2015-004256PPA-04`); the 2019 approvals (`2017-000663` OFA, ENX,
  DVA, PCA and MAP, all approved); its community plan evaluation and addendum dated Sept 17, 2019 (`ERD`);
  the revised project's environmental record opened Sept 24, 2025 (`2025-008569ENV`), and amendment
  applications filed May 6, 2026 (`2017-000663DVA-02`, `MAP-02`, `PCA-02`).

## Which could be megaprojects

By the map's scale, the candidates are the Flower Mart (up to about 3,500 homes or 2.7 million sq ft),
88 Bluxome (1,500 homes) and 655 Fourth Street (1,105 homes). All three are Central SoMa Plan sites
within about 250 m of the railyard. 725 Harrison (981,000 gsf) is a smaller fourth. Each still needs a
traced boundary from an official parcel source before it can be drawn.
