# Official-source pass, 2026-10-07: SF west and HOPE SF

Projects: Treasure Island, Stonestown, Balboa Reservoir, Parkmerced, Potrero HOPE SF,
Sunnydale HOPE SF, Schlage Lock. Every source is a public agency or body: sf.gov and
media.api.sf.gov (TIDA, Mayor, City Administrator, MOHCD and Board documents),
sfgov.legistar.com, sfplanning.org and its S3 store, citypln-m-extnl.sfgov.org (Planning's
document vault), archives.sfplanning.org, sfpuc.gov and data.sf.gov (DBI permits, dataset
i98e-djp9). "PDF p." is the page in the PDF file. Two values are derived and labelled as
such: the Stonestown Special Use District area split and the Schlage Lock agreement end date.

Status key: CONFIRMED, CORRECTED, NEW, UNCONFIRMED (looked, nothing official), CONTRADICTED.
